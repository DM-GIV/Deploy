# Contact and anti-spam review — 2026-10-15

The ordinary public contact panel now presents compact icons rather than repeating contact text. Phone and e-mail retain `tel:`/`mailto:` fallbacks and attempt clipboard copy without blocking navigation. Telegram uses the confirmed project URL. MAX copies the confirmed phone number because no verified deep link exists. VK and Yandex Dzen remain inert configuration slots until the owner confirms URLs.

This is a UX and duplication reduction, not a promise of protection from scraping: `tel:`, `mailto:` and Organization structured data remain machine-readable for truthful contact information. Legal pages retain the operator details and legally required communication method. No CAPTCHA is added for viewing contacts; ALTCHA remains limited to form submission.
